The insight · Canterbury market share

Three in ten Cantabrians are on our network. Seven in ten of their friends and family are not.

3 in 10
Canterbury connections on One NZ 11 points below our normal markets

Every other region sits between 33% and 44%. Canterbury alone sits at 30%, and it is not a coverage problem — it is a presence problem. We have never given Cantabrians a reason to switch that they could not get anywhere else.

Share is of addressable consumer mobile connections, not of population: 682,000 population x 81% = 550,000, of which 30% is ours. The $60m is 60,500 x $41 ARPU x 24 months — revenue, not margin.

Act three

One thousand Phone Dollars, both ways.

Anyone in the country can send $1,000 to anyone else in the country. If the person they send it to is not on One NZ and joins, they get $1,000 in their One Wallet — and so does the person who sent it. The next three scenes cover what that converts and what it costs.
