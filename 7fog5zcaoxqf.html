Method and definitions

How every figure here was arrived at.

Budget
A stored FY27 monthly volume multiplied by a stored rate per sale, grossed ×1.15 for GST. Both live on the OKR page, which is what makes the split below arithmetic rather than judgement.
Volume variance
(actual volume − budget volume) × budget rate
Rate variance
(actual rate − budget rate) × actual volume. This charges the joint term — extra units meeting a higher price — to rate, which is the conservative reading. The two columns sum exactly to the total.
Volume
Every interest-free sale row, deduplicated by transaction key, including rows carrying no discount.
Contribution margin
85% on incremental mobile service revenue — the network is built, so the next line is close to all contribution. Finance has not signed a figure. The workbench default is revenue at 31 months.
Account value
Total monthly plan revenue across every line on the billing account, measured per distinct account, times 31 months. The mean is $111 a month, so $3,449.
Churn gap
5.9% a year in contract against 16.1% off it. A survivorship-free cohort read — banded on an earlier base file, measured on a later one — never a single snapshot.
Attach
Extra mobile lines plus broadband activated on the same billing account within 60 days either side of the sale. The Win the Home basis.
Spend
The finance sales feed, GST-inclusive, each month taken from the latest upload that covers it in full. All six monthly totals reconcile to the A&R briefing of 17 September.
Base
The customer base file of 12 September 2026. Its totals sit under the sales feed because buyers who have since left are absent from it.
Tracked on
A&R report for spend against budget, OKR targets for the volume and rate behind it, redemption report for attach and the acquisition split, Stim Performance for the campaign reads, discount engine for the market position.
The caveat
The 1.35 times credits the discount with every sale and every attach that followed it. It is an upper bound. Targeted retention, measured against a control, returned 3.8 times.
