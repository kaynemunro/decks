05 · Oversight

AI use and human oversight

What AI does

Explains the bill. It never changes charges or takes payments.

What experts do

Review uncertain answers and improve the approved explanations.

What we watch

Accuracy, feedback and handovers, plus privacy and billing integrity.

Bill explainer · plan and prototype5
AI explains; it doesn't act. It can't change charges, apply credits or take payments. Where the bill or approved rules don't support a confident answer, it says so and offers a person. Billing experts review those uncertain answers and approve better explanations. We monitor accuracy, customer feedback and handovers, and check privacy and billing integrity throughout.

06 · Risks

Top three risks and how we manage them

Wrong explanation

Check answers against the bill. Review and correct errors.

Privacy or security

Use only the data needed, protect access, complete required reviews.

Low trust or use

Clear choice, easy feedback and a person to talk to.

Bill explainer · plan and prototype6
Wrong explanation: every answer points to the bill lines it comes from, and experts review and correct errors. Privacy or security: use the least customer data needed, protect access, and complete the required privacy and security reviews before customers use it. Low trust or use: customers choose whether to use it, can give feedback on every answer, and can always reach a person.

90days

08 · Leadership

How leaders support teams

Be clear on what changes, what stays human and who owns decisions.

Give frontline teams training, a clear handover path and hands-on support.

Protect capacity: check workload and team confidence as the trial grows.

Bill explainer · plan and prototype8
Teams need to know what changes, what stays with people, and who decides. Frontline teams get training and a clear handover path, so a customer who moves from the explainer to a person doesn't start again. Watch workload and confidence as the trial grows.
