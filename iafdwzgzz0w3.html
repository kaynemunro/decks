At $700 the match puts $3.4M behind members who were never going to leave. At $400 that drops to $2.0M, acquisition rises to $2.0M, at-risk holds at $2.4M — and the total falls $0.9M. September to October, $M incl GST.

Focus two

Keep driving attach, so the product mix carries the churn reduction

Annualised churn by product mix, cohorts classified before churn. Broadband is the strongest single lever.

A Phone Dollars sale already attaches at 31.1% against 18.4% above the line. December assumes 8% broadband attach; August ran 6.5%. Scale to 20%.

Focus three

Get members onto their first earn feature

70.5% of the active base uses none of the five add-on earn mechanics. Almost the whole churn difference sits at the first one.

Adopters self-select, so treat the 5.9pp gap as a ceiling, not proven lift.
